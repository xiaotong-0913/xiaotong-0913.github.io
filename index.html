GitHub · LinkedIn · CV

I work on multimodal AI, speech processing and emotion recognition, with an interest in human–computer and human–robot interaction. I completed my MSc (M2) in MMVAI at Université Paris-Saclay in September 2026.

My research focuses on emotion recognition and human-state analysis using speech, audio, EMG, IMU and motion signals. Previously, I worked on NLP at the Weihai Institute for Bionics, Jilin University, and on French corpus development at Shanghai International Studies University. My background combines French language and linguistics, natural language processing, and AI.

My research interests include:

  • Multimodal affective computing. My main focus is speech-based emotion recognition and human-state analysis for human–robot interaction and rehabilitation.
  • Speech and natural language processing. Multilingual NLP, corpus analysis and speech-based evidence for human-centered applications.
  • Trustworthy AI and privacy. I am interested in reliable AI systems and privacy protection in speech and multimodal applications.

I am interested in research collaborations and professional opportunities in these areas. Please see my CV or get in touch.
